**The golden rule: AI thinks, the template builds.** The AI never writes menu HTML.

thousands of pages, and it's why a wrong price can never be "hallucinated" into a menu.

## 2. Your capacity questions — straight answers

**"How many menus can 2 accounts ship in 24 hours?"**
Wrong bottleneck, brother. With the factory, menus don't consume Claude usage at all:
the engine built a menu in **6 milliseconds** (archetype mode = 0 AI tokens). On your
MacBook, 100 menus build in **under 1 second**. Ship rate is now limited by (a) finding
good prospects and (b) you sending DMs — not by any plan limit.

**"Can we drain a daily limit in 1 hour and finish 100 menus?"**
You finish 100 menus in under a minute and drain approximately **nothing**. If you want
AI-personalized data cards for all 100 (Tier-2 personalization): ~400 tokens × 100 =
~40K output tokens ≈ a few percent of one Max session. The old way (AI hand-writing
each 15KB page in chats) would have been ~450-500K tokens plus retries — that's the
plan you were imagining, and we deleted the need for it.

**"20 chats × 5 menus each — smart?"**
It works but it's the inefficient shape: 20 chats = 20 style drifts, 0 QA, and you
copy-pasting for hours. 1 orchestrator + deterministic build beats it on speed,
consistency, and your sanity. (Verified pattern: evaluator-optimizer + templating,
see RESEARCH.md.)

**"3 Max accounts — use 2 as autonomous menu farms?"**
Honest verdict: **don't build the business on it.**
- Anthropic's Consumer Terms ban "bypassing any of our systems or protective
  measures" — rate limits are protective measures, and accounts have been suspended
  for limit evasion and account sharing.
- A Claude Code team member has said owning multiple Max accounts isn't by itself
  against ToS (enforcement targets sharing/resale/evasion) — but that's a statement,
  not written policy. Getting an account banned mid-campaign would hurt the business
  far more than a slower batch.
- **And you don't need it**: the factory made menu generation ~free. One Max account
  covers research + personalization for 100+ cafes/day. If you ever truly outgrow it,
  the sanctioned scale path is the **Claude API** (Batch mode = 50% off; 100 data
  cards ≈ under $1). Keep the other accounts for teammates as your squad grows —
  that's what they're for.

**Where your Fable/Opus power SHOULD go** (this is the real 50/50 split):
- Fable: research sprints, prospect harvesting, formula upgrades, hard debugging.
- Opus/Sonnet: per-cafe data cards, DM personalization, translation checks.

## 3. The weapon research handed us: SFDA compliance 🎯

Saudi law (SFDA) requires **calorie counts on ALL menus since Jan 2019** — cafes
included — and since **1 July 2025** also caffeine content on drinks, salt-shaker
icons on high-sodium items, allergens, and activity-to-burn estimates. Paper menus
across Riyadh are legally outdated. Our pages now carry "SFDA-ready" in the pitch,
and the data format supports the fields. New DM opener:

> "منيوكم الورقي ما يغطي اشتراطات هيئة الغذاء والدواء الجديدة (السعرات + الكافيين).
> جهزنا لكم منيو إلكتروني متوافق وجاهز — شوفوه: [link]"

Competition: Foodics (500-3,400 SAR/mo, full POS — overkill for small cafes),
FineDine (~90-280 SAR/mo), budget players from 8 SAR/mo. **We don't compete on
price; we compete on done-for-you**: "send a WhatsApp photo of your paper menu,
get a finished bilingual SFDA-ready menu the same day."
