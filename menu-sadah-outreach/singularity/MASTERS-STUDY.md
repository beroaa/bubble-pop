Why the masters feel hand-made by 10 designers:
1. **A narrative WORLD, not categories.** Beyt = a house you walk through: rooms
   ("The saj is already hot / الصاج حامي وناطركن", "Under the vine", "There's
   always an extra plate", "When the windows glow"). RUSTIC = a grill house
   (tickets, stations, neon sign, sparks). The MENU IS A STORY.
2. **Story headlines per category** — warm dialect Arabic + poetic English,
   never "Espresso Bar".
3. **Hand-made decoration language**: price STAMPS (pstamp), washi TAPE,
   DOODLES, steam WISPS, ink-drawn scene SVGs (27 of them!), diary notes.
4. **Micro-animation vocabulary** (29 keyframes): word-in, ink-in, row-ink on
   scroll, dot-pop on price dots, wobble on hover, stamp-pop, twinkle, float.
5. **Multi-accent rotation** — sections cycle 4 accents (rose/gold/ochre/sage),
   not one brand color everywhere.
6. **Diary voice sprinkles** — tiny human notes between sections.
7. Real display fonts (already in v5).
