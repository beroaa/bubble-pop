# 📸 PHOTO-MISSION-50 — the Visuals-First hunt (Ibrahim's #1 order)

Mission for Mac-Claude: for EACH of the 50 cafes below, hunt their REAL product/shop/menu
photos and land them in the repo. This is the single biggest quality lever we have.

## Rules (Mayfair standard)
- Sources per cafe (in order): Instagram/TikTok handle below -> Google Maps photos -> delivery apps (Jahez/HungerStation/ToYou/Keeta) -> their website. ONLY images from the cafe itself (evidence rule — no stock, no other cafes).
- Per cafe collect 6-10 shots: 2-3 hero-worthy product close-ups, 1-2 interior/shop, 1-2 signature dish, 1 counter/menu-board if exists.
- Save to: menu-sadah-outreach/dist/assets/photos/<slug>/1.jpg ... N.jpg (JPEG, min width 1200px — upscale smaller ones with sips/Preview export or a local upscaler; crop tasteful 4:3 or 1:1).
- Write meta.json per folder: [{"file":"1.jpg","shows":"pistachio latte close-up","src":"instagram post URL"}] — provenance mandatory.
- Also grab the cafe LOGO when findable -> logo.png in same folder.
- Commit+push in batches of 10 cafes with message "PHOTO-50 batch N".

## What happens next (cloud side, already planned)
- Engine gains per-cafe photo mode: hero gallery, room-header photos, gift detail cards switch to REAL brand shots automatically when <slug>/ folder exists. Fallback stays the current art. NO menu rebuild needed on your side beyond push.
