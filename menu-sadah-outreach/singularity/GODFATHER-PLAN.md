## 🗓️ WEEK 1 (this week) — FIRST BLOOD

Goal: **1-3 cafes fully LIVE** (QR on tables, owner happy, voice-note testimonial).
Not 50 deals. One anchor client beats 945 built menus.

### Daily checklist (repeat every day, Mon-Sat)
- [ ] **Morning (AI factory):** pick today's 8-10 target cafes from the DM cockpit (best-ranked first, same district — see domino play). Print/prep their gift-page QRs.
- [ ] **Send 5-8 DMs** from the 50-DM campaign — but DMs are the KNOCK, not the weapon. Every DM ends with "I'll pass by your cafe this week."
- [ ] **3-5pm: WALK IN to 8-10 cafes.** Dead hours = owner has time. Open with the free SFDA check (below), then demo THEIR OWN gift page live on your phone. Let the owner hold the phone. That moment closes deals.
- [ ] **Hand the Golden Envelope** (below) if you have one for that cafe.
- [ ] **Evening:** WhatsApp follow-up to every owner you met ("great meeting you, here is your link again"). Log every visit + reaction in ClientOS.
- [ ] **Before anything goes live: OWNER-VERIFY GATE.** 20-min sit-down with the owner to confirm every item + price. Until then the menu is "a draft gift I built for you" — never "your menu, ready." One wrong price kills the compliance pitch forever.
- [ ] **Night (AI factory):** fix everything owners flagged, prep tomorrow's targets, update the board.

### Zero-cost weapon #1 — 🟡 Golden Envelope Drop
- Wax-sealed envelope, cafe's name in Arabic calligraphy, inside: one card with a printed QR to **their own finished masterpiece gift page** + two lines: "منيوك جاهز. امسح وشوف." (Your menu is ready. Scan and see.)
- Cost: ~2 SAR each (paper + wax stick). Start with the 10 best of the 50, hand-delivered during walk-ins. Nothing sells like an owner holding his own menu.
- Factory job: generate the 50 QR cards print-file tonight.

### Zero-cost weapon #2 — 🩺 Free SFDA Compliance Check
- Every walk-in and DM OPENS with this: "Free private check of your current menu against SFDA rules — calories mandatory since 2019, caffeine + allergens since July 2025, including digital menus. I'll send you the report in 24h, no strings."
- Service first, pitch second. NEVER shame anyone publicly. The report is honest — if they pass, say they pass (that honesty is the brand).
- Factory job: 1-page Arabic audit template; you photograph their menu in the cafe, factory fills the report same night.

### Week 1 scoreboard (be honest with yourself)
- 40-60 walk-ins → 10-15 real owner conversations → **1-3 signed + LIVE**.
- The moment client #1 is live: table QR stands on tables, launch photo, **owner voice-note testimonial** in Arabic. That voice note is the only sales asset you need for the other 945.

## ⚙️ WEEKS 2-4 — THE CONVERSION MACHINE

### 1. Get payment-legit (do this FIRST, week 2)
- **What:** Freelance license (freelance.sa), one-page Arabic agreement, invoicing with VAT line, Mada/bank transfer.
- **How:** License is online + fast. Factory drafts the Arabic agreement + invoice template in one night.
- **Cost:** ~0-200 SAR.
- **Result:** "Official invoice" closes the last 20% of every deal that a personal STC Pay account loses. You can't be #1 as a DM account.

### 2. District Domino
- **What:** Land 2-3 cafes on ONE street, then hit every neighbor with the line "X next door is live — scan it."
- **How:** Pick the district where your first client lives. All walk-ins that week stay in that district. The line must be verifiable — the neighbor can literally walk over and see the QR stand.
- **Cost:** petrol.
- **Result:** Riyadh cafes copy neighbors. One block falls, the next block is 3x easier. Repeat block by block.

### 3. Gift-a-Menu referral chain — أهدِ صاحبك منيو
- **What:** Every signed cafe gets ONE transferable pre-built masterpiece menu to gift to a cafe-owner friend, with a personal letter carrying the gifter's name.
- **How:** Factory has 945 menus in stock — matching a friend's cafe takes minutes. Founding-20 members also earn one free month per intro that signs.
- **Cost:** zero (menus already built).
- **Result:** Referrals as majlis gifting, not discount codes. Feels Saudi, feels generous, spreads itself.

### 4. Client Launch Day spotlight (with permission)
- **What:** Film each client's launch day — QR stands going on tables, owner scanning, first customer scan. WRITTEN permission first, always.
- **How:** Phone camera, 60 seconds, owner tags + reshares. Factory cuts the edit brief.
- **Cost:** zero.
- **Result:** One honest launch video beats 50 demos. Their followers are your next clients.

### 5. SFDA 60-seconds — Arabic explainer videos
- **What:** 1-2 short Arabic videos weekly. You, the actual regulation text on screen, plain talk: "Did you know calories are mandatory on your menu — even digital?"
- **How:** Factory writes scripts + on-screen text; you film face-to-camera in 10 minutes.
- **Cost:** zero.
- **Result:** You become "the compliance guy" owners forward to each other. The account has a pulse before your envelope lands.

### 6. Day-1 Ritual — Same-Day New-Item Service (start with client #1)
- **What:** Owner WhatsApps a photo + price → the dish is live on their menu, with SFDA calorie/caffeine lines, within 4 hours.
- **How:** You forward to the factory, factory updates, you confirm back with a screenshot. Make it a ceremony: "تم ✅ صحنك صار لايف."
- **Cost:** minutes.
- **Result:** The daily habit that makes canceling mean going back to reprinting menus. This ritual IS the product.

### Weeks 2-4 scoreboard
- Week 2: legit + client #2-3 live. Week 3: first domino street done, first referral gift sent. Week 4: **5-8 paying clients**, 2 launch videos posted, 4+ SFDA videos out.

## ⛔ RED LINES — we NEVER do these

1. **No fake proof.** No invented testimonials, no fake scan numbers, no "20 cafes signed" when it's 2. Real voice notes, real stats, or nothing.
2. **No fake urgency.** Founding-20 closes at 20 real clients — not before, not "last 3 spots" when 18 are open.
3. **No invented facts.** Evidence rule holds everywhere: every dish has a source, price unknown = not on the menu, owner-verify gate before anything is called "live."
4. **No spam.** Max one DM + one follow-up per cafe per campaign. After a "no": 30-day cooloff, then only new-value contact (e.g., a real SFDA change).
5. **No public shaming.** SFDA audits are private gifts. We never post "this cafe is violating the law." The Index names districts, never cafes.
6. **No pretending compliance advice is legal counsel.** We cite the regulation and implement it; we say "check with SFDA for edge cases."
7. **No going live without the owner-verify sit-down.** Ever. One wrong price costs more than ten slow deals.
