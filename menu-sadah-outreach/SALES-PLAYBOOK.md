## The 7 moves, ranked by expected payoff

### 1. The Lighthouse Play (do this FIRST)
Your first client is worth 10x their money as PROOF. Offer your top 3 green cafes
(Hintia, Ashjar, House of Matcha class) a founding-partner deal: free setup +
symbolic monthly, in exchange for: their photos, a testimonial line, and
"live client" status. After ONE signs, every future DM gains:
"شاهدوا عميلنا: menu-sadah.com/<their-page>" — real Riyadh cafe, not a demo.

### 2. In-person for the top 5 (Riyadh advantage)
DMs are for scale; the first deals close face-to-face. Visit the cafe, order
their signature dish (you KNOW it from the data), ask casually who owns the place,
then show their OWN menu on your phone. Nothing beats the owner's face when he
sees his brand already alive. Bring the drink to the conversation:
"طلبت الفلات وايت حقكم — وبنيت لكم هذا."

### 3. Timing discipline
Cafe owners live on Instagram 8–11 PM. Send DMs then. Never Friday morning.
Follow WhatsApp business etiquette: real name, real photo, LinkedIn in bio.

### 4. The 3-touch follow-up (most deals die from silence, not "no")
- Day 0: the Made-to-Stick DM (cockpit).
- Day 2: soft value nudge — "أضفنا صور حقيقية للمنيو، شكله صار أجمل — نفس الرابط."
- Day 5: the SFDA card — "من يوليو ٢٠٢٥ الكافيين إلزامي حتى للمنيو الرقمي —
  منيوكم التجريبي جاهز ومتوافق. آخر رسالة مننا، والرابط يبقى لكم هدية."
Then STOP. Dignity sells. Mark stage in ClientOS.

### 5. Voice note after first reply
The moment a cafe replies ANYTHING, answer with a 30-second voice note: calm,
Saudi, warm. Text = agency spam; voice = real person. Book a 10-min visit or call.
Never negotiate price in DM.

### 6. Simple pricing, anchored on pain
Two options only (complexity kills): Setup + monthly. Anchor against what they
already burn: menu reprints every price change + delivery-app commissions +
SFDA compliance risk. "أقل من صحن تشيزكيك يومياً."

### 7. Referral loop (after client #1)
Every signed cafe owner knows 5 other owners. Offer: a free month for every
cafe they introduce. You already have the other cafes' pages BUILT — the
"introduction" is instant: "شوف وش سوينا لجارك."
