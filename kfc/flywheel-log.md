without the log there is no self-learning, just repeated luck.

## Promotion reviews — every 25 menus

Answer in writing, update `taste-spec.md` accordingly:

1. Which dimensions fail most? → strengthen that section of the generator or
   spec.
2. What do the menus that got CLAIMED have in common that ignored ones don't?
   → promote those patterns into the spec.
3. What did first-try-pass menus share? → that's the formula working; protect it.
4. Any critic score that doesn't correlate with clients claiming/paying? →
   the scorecard is measuring the wrong thing; fix the scorecard, not the menus.
