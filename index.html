The Backstory

Twenty-five, London-based, and one semester from a business degree — Ibrahim Sharbatly carries seventy-five years of family enterprise into its next generation. By day he heads business development at Elite Town Real Estate, moving across the full property lifecycle: investment, development, leasing and management.

By night he builds. He designed and shipped the Elite Town website himself, launched Menu Sadah — bilingual digital menus for Gulf cafés — and grew nafas.practice, a science-backed breath-work brand, past a thousand followers. In September 2025 he earned an MIT xPRO certificate in designing and building AI products.
